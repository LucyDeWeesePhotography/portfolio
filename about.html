The Heart Behind the Camera

Hi, I am Lucy DeWeese

I am a photographer, graphic designer, and mother of three who believes the most meaningful moments are often the simplest ones. The laughter, the little details, the quiet connections, and the love shared between people are the stories I want to preserve.

My photography is centered around capturing people as they truly are and preserving the genuine emotions that make each story unique. I believe photographs are a gift we leave for ourselves and for the people we love, preserving the feelings and moments that time cannot replace.

My Story Through Time

1997 My love for photography began when I was in sixth grade. I found myself drawn to capturing the people around me and preserving the everyday moments that felt special. I loved looking back at those photographs because they held pieces of my life that I never wanted to forget. Even at a young age, I understood how quickly moments pass and how photographs allow us to hold on to the memories that matter most.

As I grew older, photography remained a quiet passion in my life. I began photographing friends and exploring the art of storytelling through images, continuing to develop my skills and my eye for meaningful moments.

2017 Losing my mother, Deusa, changed the way I viewed photographs forever. They became more than images. They became reminders of love, memories, and the precious moments we share with the people who matter most.

2019 My daughter, Luna Deusa, was born and brought a new chapter of joy and purpose into my life. Her arrival reminded me that even after seasons of loss, beautiful new beginnings are possible.

During that season, while photographing my daughter and studying at BYU–Idaho, I felt inspired to pursue photography more intentionally. What began as a personal passion slowly became a way to help others preserve their own stories and memories.

My studies in Web Design and Development, along with my art and design courses at BYU–Idaho, helped shape my creative vision. Through tools like Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, Lightroom, and InDesign, I developed a deeper appreciation for combining creativity, design, and storytelling.

Today, I have the privilege of capturing the moments that families may one day treasure most. My goal is to create photographs that preserve love, connection, and memories that can be cherished for generations.
