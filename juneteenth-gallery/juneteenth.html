Thank You!

Thank you to everyone who came out and supported our Juneteenth celebration! It was a wonderful event, and it was inspiring to see our community come together in such a meaningful way.

We hope everyone left with a deeper understanding of the history and significance of Juneteenth through the displays, artwork, music, and the messages shared by our speakers. The evening was filled with opportunities to learn, reflect, and connect with one another.

A special thank you to Vendella for coordinating so many details and creating such a beautiful atmosphere for the event. We are also grateful to everyone who contributed food, helped serve, shared their artistic talents, provided music, and volunteered their time to make the evening possible.

As we reflect on what we learned together, we hope we can carry the spirit of Juneteenth with us throughout the year, continuing to build understanding, unity, and appreciation for one another.

Thank you again for being part of this special evening. We look forward to many more opportunities to gather, learn, and celebrate together in the future.
